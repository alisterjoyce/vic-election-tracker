Privacy

The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no tracking or analytics. Your settings stay in your own browser.

Last updated 23 Nov 2026.

What the site stores, in your browser only

  • Settings: your light or dark theme choice (localStorage, key theme).
  • Your watchlist: the seats you star for "My seats" (localStorage).
  • Your prediction: the seats you change on "Build your own prediction" (localStorage, key vet-predict). Clear it with "Reset to models".
  • Offline copies: when you open the site, your browser keeps copies of its pages, data files and photos (a service worker's cache) so the site loads faster and works offline.

All of this stays on your device. The site has no server of its own to send it to, and you can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. The site works without it.

Your location

"Find my seat" on the home page asks your browser for your location only when you tap it. The location is used in your browser to work out which district you're in, and is not sent to this site or anyone else, and not stored. You can decline, and search by suburb or postcode instead.

No tracking or analytics

The site uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no social-media widgets. It doesn't know who you are or what you looked at. Sharing an image uses your own device's share sheet, or downloads the image.

Hosting and other servers

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub logs each request; GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (GitHub Pages: data collection; GitHub Privacy Statement). The tracker's author has no access to those logs.
  • cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) serves the chart, map and share-image libraries some pages load; your browser requests them from there, as from any server.
  • Links to other sites (the VEC, news outlets, party pages) are governed by those sites' own privacy policies once you follow them.

Questions

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
