BludgerTrack and Ace Strategies are read once a day, electiontracker.au at every refresh. electiontracker.au's figures are CC BY 4.0; the others belong to their publishers, linked in the table.

How this site's average works: every statewide poll on the polls list except those it leaves out (below), placed at the midpoint of its fieldwork, is averaged with Gaussian-kernel weights (a 21-day standard deviation, so polls more than 63 days away count for nothing) multiplied by the square root of its sample size over 1,000. Left out: polls commissioned by a party, union or advocacy group, and polls with a sample under 800 or none published, following electiontracker.au's published poll rules (rules D and G); they stay on the list, greyed, with the reason. Who commissioned each poll comes from electiontracker.au's poll ledger where it lists the poll, otherwise from the client Wikipedia names; a poll whose commissioner isn't stated stays in. Unlike electiontracker.au, this site makes no case-by-case exceptions and keeps no pollster allowlist. The latest figure uses only polls up to the newest one, so it is dated at that poll's fieldwork midpoint. The 2PP averages each poll's published two-party figure, and independents count in Others, as on the home page's chart. The small figures under this site's row are each average's 95% range: the standard error of the weighted average, from each poll's sampling error (its sample size) and from how far the polls in the window spread around the average beyond that (pooled with the spread across the whole series), times 1.96. It leaves out any error that every pollster shares.
