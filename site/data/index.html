Licence and credit

Unless a file says otherwise below, the data is licensed Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). Reuse it freely, crediting: Vic Election 2026 tracker.

  • Electoral boundaries, districts, regions, 2022 results and the how-to-vote register come from the Victorian Electoral Commission; credit the VEC.
  • Margins, classifications and lower-house candidate lists come from the ABC election guide; credit ABC News. The tracker stores facts only and links to the ABC's pages.
  • Data drawn from Wikipedia (polls, some candidates, 2022 swings) is CC BY-SA 4.0: share those parts alike.
  • Photos, headlines and how-to-vote cards belong to their owners and aren't covered by this licence.
  • Election-night results from the VEC's media feed are not offered here: that feed is covered by the VEC's own terms of use.
