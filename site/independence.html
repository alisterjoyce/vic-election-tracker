Independence

Who runs this tracker, and what it doesn't do.

Last updated 23 Nov 2026.

Who runs it

The tracker is an independent project run by Alister Joyce in Melbourne, who authorises its content. Its code and data are public on GitHub.

Not affiliated with anyone

The tracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by or working for any political party or candidate, or the Victorian Electoral Commission (VEC). Nor is it affiliated with the ABC or the other publishers whose information it uses and credits. The VEC runs the election; for anything about your enrolment or your vote, go to the VEC.

No paid content

  • No advertising, sponsorship, paid placement or affiliate links. Nobody pays to appear on the site, to be listed first or to be left out.
  • Betting prices are shown as information about the market, not as advice or encouragement to bet.
  • How-to-vote cards are shown as each party or candidate registered them with the VEC, labelled as their recommendation, never ours.

How it stays neutral

  • No recommendations. The site never tells anyone how to vote.
  • Neutral order. Candidates are listed in the VEC's ballot order (or by surname where there is none), and parties in one fixed alphabetical order, never by polls, odds or seats held. See Order of candidates and parties.
  • The same rules for everyone. Candidate profiles follow one set of rules: facts only, in our own words, every fact sourced, no commentary, praise or criticism.
  • Sources shown. Every figure and fact links to where it came from, and projections made by this site (the poll of polls, uniform-swing seats, election-night calls) are labelled as such, with their method on the About page.

Corrections

Spot an error or an unfair omission? Open an issue on GitHub. Corrections are made whoever they favour.
