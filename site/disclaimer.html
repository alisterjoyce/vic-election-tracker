Unofficial information

This is an unofficial tracker. It gathers published information about the 2026 Victorian state election and links to its sources. It is not the Victorian Electoral Commission (VEC). The VEC's own website is the authority on enrolment, candidates, ballot papers, where and when to vote, and results: vec.vic.gov.au.

Accuracy

The tracker is refreshed automatically four times a day and checked by hand, but it can be out of date or wrong: sources change, automatic matching can make mistakes, and a refresh can fail. Information is provided as is, without any warranty. Check anything important with the original source, which is linked wherever the information appears.

Projections, models and odds

  • The poll of polls, its ranges, the uniform-swing seat projection and the election-night calls are this site's own calculations from published figures. They are estimates, not predictions, and the method is on the About page.
  • Seat models are other organisations' work, credited and linked where shown.
  • Betting prices are shown as information about the market, as at the date given. They are not advice or encouragement to bet.
  • Election-night figures are the VEC's, read from its results pages; a seat "called by this site" is our projection, not the VEC's declaration.

Other people's content

Headlines, photos, how-to-vote cards, policy statements and seat models belong to their owners and are credited and linked. A link to another site isn't an endorsement of it, and the tracker isn't responsible for other sites' content. How-to-vote cards are each party's or candidate's own recommendation.

Not advice

Nothing on this site is advice about how to vote, or legal, financial or betting advice.
